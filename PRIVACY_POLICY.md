# Privacy Policy for Todo - New Tab

**Last Updated: February 3, 2026**

## Overview

Todo - New Tab ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The Extension operates entirely locally on your device. All your todo items, preferences, and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage.

## What Data is Stored Locally

The Extension stores the following data **only on your device**:

1. **Todo Items**: Your task list, including task text and completion status
2. **Theme Preference**: Your selected theme mode (auto/light/dark)
3. **Filter State**: Your last selected filter view (all/active/completed)

This data is stored using Chrome's local storage API and **never leaves your device**.

## Permissions

The Extension requests the following permission:

- **storage**: Used to save your todo items and preferences locally on your device

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does **NOT**:
- Connect to any external servers
- Use any analytics services
- Include any tracking code
- Share data with third parties
- Display advertisements

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data is as secure as your device
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No accounts or authentication required
- No data breaches possible from our end

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- You can view all your data within the Extension
- You can delete individual items or clear all data
- You can uninstall the Extension at any time, which removes all local data

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
